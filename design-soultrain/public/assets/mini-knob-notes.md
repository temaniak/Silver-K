# Mini knob artwork

Saved asset: `mini-knob.png`. Created with the built-in imagegen tool, transparent background. Replaces the blue screwdriver trim-pot artwork in active Arp/Options controls.

Final prompt:

One standalone raster UI asset: a tiny finger-turnable synthesizer knob in the style of compact Eurorack modules and Korg Volca controls. Exact straight top view, round low-profile black rubber/plastic cap with fine radial knurling/ribs visible around its rim, slightly recessed flat charcoal top, one clear slim white pointer stripe pointing straight toward twelve o'clock. This is a small physical knob, not a screwdriver trim pot. No blue material, no screw or screw slot, no LED ring, no scale or tick marks, no letters or numbers. Crisp simple silhouette, subtle neutral metallic-gray highlights on ridges, matte finish. It must read clearly at 28px display size. Transparent background, no surrounding object, no cast shadow outside the round knob. Single centered knob fills about 85% of the square canvas. Production UI sprite, no perspective tilt.
